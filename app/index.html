Roadmap to the platinum

Six stages, in order, built from the official descriptions. The story comes first — it unlocks the map, the partner, the apps and the robbery tools everything else needs. Nothing outside the story is missable except the one trophy flagged below, but keep a manual save at the start of every chapter: GTA VI has no mission replay.

    Missable warnings

    Only one trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude. Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 disappears once that chapter ends, and there is no mission replay. Do it the morning after the mission Down to Earth, and keep a manual save from the start of Chapter 1 until the trophy pops. Every other trophy stays available after the credits — side-story finales, collectibles, activities and the 100% checklist all remain open.

    • Save before every chapter. 15 manual save slots plus one autosave: keep one at the start of each chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission.
    • Race Against Yago is time-sensitive. This Chapter 3 race in Yago’s street-racing strand carries an hourglass icon and the strand counts toward Strangers & Freaks. Save first, then race.
    • Never use cheats on your trophy save. Cheats should switch off both trophies and saving.

    Sources & how this guide was built

    Official names, descriptions, grades and hidden flags come from the official trophy list as published on Exophase, cross-checked against PSNProfiles (which also supplies the progress counts PlayStation tracks, such as 42 Shooting Range challenges). Guide details are drawn from Output Lag and KeenGamer, with GTA Vice City Hub as a further check, and each trophy’s guide below names the sources its facts come from. Every guide is written in our own words for this entry — sources are cited, never copied. Where a detail is still unconfirmed on launch week, the guide says so rather than guessing. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork or game screenshot is used anywhere in this guide.

    This app is fully offline: it loads only its own files, fetches nothing at run time except its own trophy data, and stores your checklist in your browser’s local storage. Export downloads a JSON file you can keep or import back later.
